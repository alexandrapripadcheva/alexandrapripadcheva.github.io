Professional Summary

My research combines high-performance numerical methods with large-scale administrative and household microdata (HMDA, PSID, Zillow, Redfin, FHFA) to analyze housing ladder frictions, mortgage markets disequilibrium, and wealth inequality.

Research Interests

Computational Macroeconomics: Heterogeneous-agent life-cycle models, housing & mortgage markets, multi-asset portfolio choice
Structural Econometrics: Dynamic programming, state-space discretization, numerical estimation & inference bias
High-Performance Computing: Parallel computing (Fortran/OpenMP), numerical optimization, high-dimensional state space models
